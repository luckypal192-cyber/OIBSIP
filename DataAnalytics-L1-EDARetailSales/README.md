# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Oasis Infobyte – Data Analytics Internship

### Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales
dataset to identify sales trends, customer behaviour patterns, product
performance, regional distribution, and actionable business insights.

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and
discover meaningful patterns that can support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The analysis was performed using the Superstore retail sales dataset.

The dataset contains information related to orders, customers, products,
sales, profit, discount, regions, and other retail-related attributes.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Dataset Inspection

The dataset was inspected for:

- Number of rows and columns
- Column names
- Data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Mean, median, mode, and standard deviation were calculated for the numerical
variables.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly sales were analyzed and visualized using a line chart to identify
changes in sales performance over time.

### 4. Quarterly Sales Trend

Quarterly sales were analyzed to identify seasonal and long-term sales
patterns.

### 5. Customer Segment Analysis

Order distribution was analyzed across:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 6. Region-wise Analysis

Order distribution was analyzed across the four regions:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

### 7. Top 10 Best-Selling Products

The top 10 products were identified based on total sales revenue.

### 8. Revenue by Product Category

Total revenue was compared across:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

### 9. Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created to analyze relationships between numerical
variables.

### 10. Discount vs Profit Analysis

Average profit was analyzed across different discount levels to understand
the impact of discounting on profitability.

## Key Insights

- The Consumer segment has the highest number of orders.
- The West region has the highest order volume.
- Technology is the highest-revenue product category.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the highest-selling product by
  sales revenue.
- Sales and Profit show a moderate positive relationship.
- Discount and Profit show a negative relationship.
- Higher discount levels are associated with substantially lower average
  profit in the analyzed data.
- Monthly and quarterly sales show considerable fluctuations, with an
  overall upward movement in the later period.

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize high-performing products in inventory and promotional planning.
2. Control excessive discounting to protect profit margins.
3. Continue focusing on high-performing Technology products.
4. Develop targeted marketing strategies for regions with lower order volume.
5. Plan inventory and marketing activities around historically strong sales
   periods.

## Conclusion

The EDA provides valuable insights into sales trends, customer segments,
regional performance, product performance, and profitability.

The findings demonstrate how exploratory data analysis can support
data-driven business decisions by identifying high-performing products,
strong sales regions, revenue-generating categories, and the potential
negative impact of excessive discounting.
